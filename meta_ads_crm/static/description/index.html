Quick 3-Step Setup

  1. Connect Meta Account: Go to Settings > Meta Ads, configure App Credentials, and authorize your Facebook Page & Instagram account.
  2. Configure Lead Forms: Select target Facebook Lead Forms and map questions to your Odoo CRM lead attributes.
  3. Start Capturing Leads: Enjoy real-time incoming leads generated directly into your Odoo CRM pipeline!
